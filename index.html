About

I work on language and multimodal intelligence, with a focus on knowledge graph completion, low-resource intent detection, few-shot multi-label learning, and agentic research workflows.

My recent work studies how models can retrieve, rerank, propagate relational knowledge, and learn from sparse supervision across text, vision, and structured knowledge. I also build Research Harness, an agent-first workflow layer for long-horizon scientific work with persistent state and provenance.
